## 1. Purpose

This Roles and Responsibilities Matrix defines governance, management, development, deployment, and operational roles for the Laundry Shop Management System project. It ensures accountability, alignment with SDLC principles (SAD 10th Edition), and clear Adminship of deliverables across all phases of the project lifecycle.
