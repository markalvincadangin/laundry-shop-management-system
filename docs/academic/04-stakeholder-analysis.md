## 1. Purpose of the Stakeholder Analysis

The purpose of this stakeholder analysis is to identify individuals and groups who have an interest in or influence over the Laundry Shop Management System. This analysis informs the project manager and sponsor of stakeholder expectations, potential risks, and the actions required to ensure stakeholder engagement prior to and during detailed project planning.

## 2. Stakeholder Analysis Matrix

| Stakeholder | Their interest or requirement for the project | What the project needs from them | Perceived attitudes and/or risks | Actions to take |
|-------------|-----------------------------------------------|----------------------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------|
| **Admin** | - Improved operational efficiency<br>- Reduced manual record keeping<br>- Accurate daily, monthly, and yearly sales reports<br>- Better control over business operations | - Approval of project scope and requirements<br>- Participation in interviews and review meetings<br>- Validation of system outputs and reports | - Concern about system complexity and Data Security<br>- High interest and strong support for the system<br>- Risk of limited availability due to daily operational duties | - Schedule regular review and approval meetings<br>- Validate requirements and reports early<br>- Involve Admin in major decision points<br>- Keep system simple and practical<br>- Present clear prototypes for feedback |
| **Staff** | - Easier tracking of laundry orders<br>- Reduced errors during order handling<br>- Simple and easy-to-use system interface | - Accurate entry of laundry orders and status updates<br>- Feedback on workflow and usability during analysis and prototyping<br>- Participation in observation and validation | - Generally supportive of the project<br>- Possible resistance to changes in routine or unfamiliar technology<br>- Fear of making mistakes using a computer | - Conduct process walkthroughs with staff<br>- Provide basic system orientation and guidance<br>- Gather usability feedback during prototype reviews |
| **Customers** | - Accurate handling of laundry items<br>- Timely completion of laundry services<br>- Clear communication regarding order status and pickup | - Acceptance of system-generated receipts or notifications<br>- Indirect feedback on service quality and system effectiveness | - Neutral to positive attitude toward system changes<br>- Risk of dissatisfaction if errors or delays continue during transition | - Ensure system improves order accuracy<br>- Minimize service disruption during implementation<br>- Communicate service process clearly to customers |
| **System Development Team** | - Clear and complete business requirements<br>- Timely feedback from stakeholders<br>- Access to users for clarification and validation | - Proper analysis, design, and documentation of the system<br>- Delivery of the system according to agreed scope and schedule | - Risk of requirement changes or delayed feedback<br>- Risk of schedule impact due to stakeholder availability | - Establish clear communication channels<br>- Document and confirm requirements formally<br>- Define review milestones and approval checkpoints |
