## 1. Introduction
This case study documents the current operational practices of Faith Laundry Shop with the objective of identifying existing business problems and determining opportunities for improvement through the use of an information system.

The study is based on a semi-structured interview and on-site observation conducted with the business owner and staff. The findings of this case study serve as the foundation for subsequent systems analysis and design activities.

---

## 2. Business Profile

### 2.1 Business Overview
Faith Laundry Shop is a small-scale laundry service business established in 2022 and located in Ilaya, Tabuc Suba Jaro, Iloilo City. The business has been operating for approximately three (3) years and caters to local customers requiring regular laundry services.

Operations are managed by the owner with the assistance of one staff member. Both individuals are directly involved in daily business activities.

---

### 2.2 Services Offered
Faith Laundry Shop provides the following services:
- Washing
- Drying
- Folding

These services are offered on a per-load basis, depending on the weight and condition of the laundry items submitted by customers.

---

## 3. Current Business Environment

### 3.1 Operational Setup
Daily operations at Faith Laundry Shop are primarily manual. When customers bring in their laundry, the staff measures the total weight in kilograms and sorts the items according to customer preferences, such as separating colored and white clothing.

The laundry is then washed, dried, and folded using available washing machines. Once completed, the items are placed in labeled bags bearing the customer’s name and prepared for pickup.

Receipts or claim stubs are issued to customers containing:
- Customer name
- Contact number
- Transaction date
- Total payment amount
- Laundry shop name
- Authorized signature

In most cases, payment is collected upon pickup rather than at drop-off.

---

### 3.2 Pricing Structure
Pricing at Faith Laundry Shop is computed on a per-load basis:

- One (1) load costs **PHP 120**
- One load covers up to **eight (8) kilograms**
- Laundry exceeding 8 kg is charged as an additional load

In special cases, additional charges may apply:
- Extra fabric conditioner requests
- Extended washing time due to excessive dirt

For extended washing time, an additional charge of **PHP 1 per extra minute** of machine use is added.

All pricing computations are performed manually by staff at the time of transaction.

---

### 3.3 Technology Usage
The business relies almost entirely on paper-based tools:
- Physical tags
- Logbooks
- Manual notebooks for payments and sales

Records are kept for approximately one (1) month before being archived for storage. No computerized or automated system is currently used to manage operations or generate reports.

---

## 4. Problem Identification

### 4.1 Observed Problems
Based on the interview and observation, the following operational problems were identified:

#### Manual and Time-Consuming Record Keeping
Recording laundry orders, payments, and sales manually requires significant effort and time from both the owner and staff.

#### Occasional Order Mix-Ups
During peak hours or when handling multiple rush orders, clothes may occasionally be mixed, increasing the risk of errors and customer dissatisfaction.

#### Limited Order Tracking Capability
Laundry status tracking depends on logbooks and physical inspection, which can lead to delays or inaccuracies.

#### Lack of Automated Reporting
Income tracking is manual, and the owner does not have access to automated daily, monthly, or yearly reports.

#### Operational Dependency on Machinery
Washing machine malfunctions cause significant stress and financial impact due to downtime and costly repairs. Although a system cannot prevent machine failure, better tracking and visibility can help manage operations during downtime.

---

### 4.2 Root Causes
The primary causes of these problems include:
- Heavy reliance on manual processes
- Lack of system automation
- Dependence on human memory for order tracking

These factors limit efficiency, increase error likelihood, and restrict access to timely business information.

---

## 5. Business Impact
The identified problems negatively affect operational efficiency and reliability. Manual record keeping slows service delivery and increases the risk of inaccuracies. Order mix-ups can lead to customer complaints and loss of trust.

The absence of automated reporting makes it difficult for the owner to evaluate business performance, compare income across periods, and make informed operational decisions.

---

## 6. Opportunity for System Improvement
The findings indicate a strong opportunity to improve operations through a Laundry Shop Management System that supports:

- Digital recording of laundry orders
- Centralized transaction history
- Automated computation of laundry charges
- SMS or digital notifications for customers
- Real-time order tracking using a unique reference number

Providing reliable order visibility reduces reliance on verbal inquiries and manual logbooks, improving service efficiency and accuracy.

---

## 7. Project Justification
Developing an information system for Faith Laundry Shop is justified by the need to streamline operations, reduce errors, and support better business decision-making.

The business owner has expressed willingness to adopt a simple computer-based system. Implementing a system-based solution will provide long-term benefits by improving efficiency, enhancing customer service, and supporting sustainable business growth.

---

## 8. Conclusion
This case study examined the current operations of Faith Laundry Shop and identified key challenges related to manual processes, limited tracking capabilities, and lack of automated reporting.

The findings demonstrate a clear need for a system-based solution. This document provides a solid foundation for proceeding with requirements definition, system design, and implementation activities.
